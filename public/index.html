How to Use LocalDrop LAN

Guide

High-speed, zero-cloud file and folder transfer across your devices.

1

Connect to the Same Network

Make sure both devices (PC, Mac, iPhone, Android) are connected to the same Wi-Fi router or local Ethernet switch.

2

Open on Other Devices

Click the QR Code button in the header and scan it with your phone's camera, or visit http://filetrans.local:8080 on your browser.

3

Select a Peer to Send

Devices appear automatically on the radar. Click on any device to choose individual files, multiple files, or whole directories to send.

4

Fast Direct Streaming

The receiver confirms the prompt and the file streams at full hardware speed directly into their downloads. Zero cloud limits, 100% private.

Pro Tips:

• Folder Sync: Switch to the Folder Sync tab to send deep directory hierarchies directly without zip overhead.

• Device Name: You can customize your device name right from the top right input.

• Auto Updates: Keep your app updated anytime by clicking the "Update" button in the header.
